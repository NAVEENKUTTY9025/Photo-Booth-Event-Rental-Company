Our Story

Founded with Passion · Est. 2018

Born from a Passion for Celebration

SnapJoy began in 2018 with a simple mission: to bring the joy of a premium photo booth experience to every celebration. What started as a single open-air booth at a friend's wedding quickly grew into a full-service event rental company trusted by hundreds of couples, planners, and brands.

We believe that the best memories are made when people let loose, have fun, and capture the moment. That's why we've invested in the finest equipment, the most elegant backdrops, and a team that treats every event like it's the most important one of the year.

From intimate backyard parties to grand corporate galas, we bring the same level of care, creativity, and polish to every event we touch.
